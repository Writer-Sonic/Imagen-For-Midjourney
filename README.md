# Imagen AI Image & Art Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Imagen AI Image & Art ("Imagen," "we," "our," or "us") collects, how we use it, when it is shared, and what choices you have. This Privacy Policy applies to the Imagen iOS app and related services.

If you use the app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account Information
When you create or use an account, we may collect:

- Name
- Email address
- User ID and account identifiers
- Sign-in provider information, such as Sign in with Apple or Google sign-in details

We use this information to create and manage your account, authenticate you, sync your content, provide customer support, and secure the service.

### 1.2 Prompts and User Content
When you use Imagen, we may collect and process:

- Text prompts you submit
- Images you upload
- Images you generate
- Reference images you attach for editing or generation
- Photos you upload to train a custom model
- Metadata associated with your requests and generated content

We use this information to provide core app functionality, including image generation, image editing, custom model training, content moderation, history, syncing, and support.

### 1.3 Device and Technical Information
We may collect:

- Device type and model
- Operating system and app version
- IP address
- Device identifiers and app instance identifiers
- Language, region, and configuration information
- Diagnostic and crash information
- Security and fraud-prevention signals

We use this information to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the app.

### 1.4 Usage Information
We may collect information about how you use the app, such as:

- Screens viewed
- Features used
- Taps, interactions, and session activity
- Subscription and entitlement status
- Purchase and restore events
- Error events and performance data

We use this information for analytics, reliability, abuse prevention, customer support, and product improvement.

### 1.5 Purchase and Subscription Information
If you make purchases, start trials, or restore purchases, we may collect and process:

- Subscription status
- Product identifiers
- Purchase timestamps
- Entitlement status
- App user ID used for subscription management

We do not directly store your full payment card details. Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store.

### 1.6 Advertising and Attribution Information
If enabled, we may collect limited attribution-related data to measure App Store advertising performance, including Apple Search Ads attribution data and related campaign performance information.

Where required, we request permission through App Tracking Transparency before using data for tracking or attribution purposes that requires that permission.

## 2. How We Collect Information

We collect information:

- Directly from you, when you create an account, enter prompts, upload images, or contact support
- Automatically from your device and app usage
- From Apple, when you purchase subscriptions through the App Store
- From service providers that help us operate the app

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide image generation, editing, and custom model training
- Authenticate users and manage accounts
- Store and sync prompts, uploads, and generated images
- Manage subscriptions, purchases, and entitlements
- Provide customer support
- Detect abuse, fraud, unauthorized activity, and policy violations
- Monitor performance, fix bugs, and improve reliability
- Analyze usage and improve the product
- Measure marketing and App Store campaign effectiveness
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

## 4. AI Processing and Third-Party Sharing

To provide image generation, editing, and related AI features, Imagen may send certain data to third-party AI service providers.

Depending on the feature you use, this may include:

- Your text prompt
- Selected model choice
- Uploaded reference images
- Uploaded photos used for custom model training
- Technical request metadata necessary to complete the request

The third-party AI providers used by Imagen may include:

- OpenAI
- Google
- Other AI processing providers used to operate Imagen's features

Before sending personal data to a third-party AI service, Imagen presents an in-app disclosure and consent flow.

### 4.2 Other Service Providers
We may also share data with service providers that help us operate Imagen, such as providers for:

- Authentication
- Cloud database and storage
- Push notifications
- Analytics
- Crash reporting
- Subscription management
- Customer support and infrastructure monitoring
- Advertising attribution

These providers process data on our behalf or as necessary to provide their services to us.

### 4.3 Services We Use
Based on the current app implementation, Imagen may use third-party services such as:

- Firebase

## 5. Public Sharing of Content

Generated images or other user content are not publicly displayed by default unless shared through app features, published to a public gallery, or otherwise made public through your use of the service.

If Imagen offers public showcase or gallery features, content you choose to submit to those features may be visible to other users.

## 6. Tracking

Imagen does not use App Tracking Transparency for core app functionality.

If we use tracking for advertising attribution or similar purposes on iOS, we will request permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt before engaging in tracking that requires that permission.

You can deny tracking permission and still use the app.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain personal data and user content only for as long as necessary to:

- Provide the service
- Maintain account history and subscriptions
- Resolve disputes and provide support
- Meet legal, tax, accounting, and security obligations
- Enforce our terms and prevent abuse

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of data and the purpose for which it was collected.

If you request deletion of your account, we will delete or de-identify your personal data and content within a reasonable period, except where we are required or permitted by law to retain certain data.

## 8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data

- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
- Object to or restrict certain processing

You can request deletion or exercise your rights through the app’s support channel or the account/data controls provided in the app.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your data, including secure transmission, access controls, and monitoring.

However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Imagen is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will delete it.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Your information may be processed and stored in countries other than your own, including countries where our service providers operate. By using Imagen, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in those countries, subject to applicable safeguards.
